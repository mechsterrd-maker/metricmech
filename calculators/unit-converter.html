Engineering Unit Conversion

Engineers work across metric and imperial systems daily — drawings in mm, supplier specs in inches, US customers asking for psi. A reliable converter prevents costly mistakes.

Critical Engineering Conversions

  • 1 inch = 25.4 mm (exact)
  • 1 mm = 0.03937 inch
  • 1 lb = 0.4536 kg
  • 1 kg = 2.205 lb
  • 1 psi = 6.895 kPa = 0.0689 bar
  • 1 MPa = 145.04 psi
  • 1 Nm = 0.7376 lb·ft
  • 1 HP = 0.7457 kW
  • °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32

Why Unit Errors Are Catastrophic

The Mars Climate Orbiter (1999) was lost because Lockheed Martin used pound-seconds for thrust while NASA used newton-seconds. Cost: ₹275 crore mission, total loss.

Common engineering unit errors:

  • Pressure units: psi vs bar vs MPa vs kPa
  • Torque units: Nm vs lb·ft vs kgf·m
  • Energy units: J vs cal vs BTU
  • Drawing tolerance: ±0.05 mm vs ±0.05" — 25× difference!

Best Practices

  • Always include units on calculations and drawings — never bare numbers.
  • For multinational projects, standardize on one unit system. Mention SI units (kg, m, s, A, K, mol, cd) when in doubt.
  • Cross-check critical values in two systems — catches transcription errors.
  • Use parameterized templates so values can be unit-flipped without manual conversion.
