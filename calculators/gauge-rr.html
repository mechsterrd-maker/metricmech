Cg = 0.2 × Tolerance / (6 × s)
Cgk = (0.1 × Tolerance − |Bias|) / (3 × s)
%EV = 6s / Tolerance × 100
Pass criteria: Cg ≥ 1.33 and Cgk ≥ 1.33
