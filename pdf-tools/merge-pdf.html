Merge PDF Files Without Uploading Them

Most online PDF mergers upload your files to their servers. For a manufacturing engineer that is usually a non-starter: customer drawings sit under NDA, inspection reports carry part numbers and pricing, and many quality systems explicitly forbid uploading controlled documents to third-party services. This tool merges PDFs entirely inside your browser using JavaScript — the files are read locally, combined locally, and the merged PDF is saved locally. Nothing crosses the network.

Typical uses on the shop floor

  • FAI packs: combine the ballooned drawing, AS9102 Forms 1–3, CMM report, and material certs into one submission PDF.
  • PPAP submissions: merge PSW, control plan, process flow, MSA studies and capability reports in the order your customer's SQE expects.
  • RFQ packages: put the drawing, specification and quantity breakdown into a single file per vendor.
  • Document control: consolidate revision history — old revision plus ECN plus new revision — into one controlled record.

How to merge

  1. Drop your PDF files onto the zone above (or click to browse).
  2. Use the ↑ ↓ buttons to set the page order — the files merge top to bottom.
  3. Click Merge PDFs and the combined file downloads immediately.

Limits and notes

Because everything runs on your machine, size limits are your device's memory rather than a server quota — hundred-page drawing packs merge fine on an ordinary laptop. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before merging. Scanned pages, vector drawings and mixed page sizes (A4 report pages plus A1 drawing sheets) are all preserved exactly as they are — merging never re-compresses or rasterises your content.
