What are you stuck on?

Specific beats general. A question with the part, the standard, the numbers, and what you already tried gets answered within hours. A vague one usually gets nothing.

Write it the way you would ask a senior engineer standing next to the machine. This becomes the page title, so make it searchable.

Before you post

  1. Search the forum — someone may have hit this already.
  2. Check the calculators if it is a number you need, not a judgement.
  3. Strip anything your employer or customer would not want public — part numbers, drawing numbers, customer names.

What gets removed

Recruitment posts, vendor advertising, homework dumps with no attempt shown, and anything that leaks a customer's confidential drawing. Everything else stays, even if the answer turns out to be "your print is wrong."

Formatting

**bold**, *italic*, `code`, triple backticks for blocks, and - for bullets. Plain URLs link themselves.
